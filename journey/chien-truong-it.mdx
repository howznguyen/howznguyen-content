---
title: "Chiến Trường IT"
date: "2014-09-01"
description: "The first forum of my own: free hosting, no backup, no staging, and no idea how much I was risking."
---

In September 2014 I put up a forum of my own, Chiến Trường IT, on XenForo and free hosting. It was less a community than a laboratory: install a plugin, try a theme, break something, fix it.

There was no local environment, no staging, no Git and no rollback. I edited files straight on the host through File Manager or FTP, and I did not know what a backup was. Once I took the whole forum down for nearly a day and could not find where it broke, so I fixed it the only way I knew: change one thing, refresh, try again.

Learning PHP came out of that, because XenForo is written in it. So did the difference between a site that opens and a site that stays up.
