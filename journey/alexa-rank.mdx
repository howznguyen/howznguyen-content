In August 2021, after redesigning iTrum on XenForo into something closer to a tech magazine, the numbers moved: Alexa put the forum at 670,744 globally and 5,933 in Vietnam, up from 985,992 ninety days earlier.

Alexa is an estimate, not a headcount, and it never paid a bill. But for a forum that a handful of us wrote for and moderated ourselves, watching the line move was the first time the work looked like a result.
