---
title: "AI becomes the direction"
date: "2024-08-01"
description: "August 2024: backend work on LLM features, and a decision at the end of the year to make AI the long-term path."
---

AI first appeared for me at university, in Python assignments about data. In August 2024 it became the job: backend work on LLM features, data flows and databases for a real product.

The turning point came later that year, when I decided to start a master's. Until then AI was a branch I was trying out; after that it was the direction, the place where full stack, backend and data experience could meet.

I did not leave software engineering to move into something else. AI became a layer on top of what I had already built.
