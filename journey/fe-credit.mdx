I joined FE Credit in July 2024, coming out of three years of outsourcing work at Cube System. It was the first product company I had worked in: same job title, different world.

Outsourcing teaches you to deliver to someone else's definition of done. Here the product was ours to keep, and the domain was new to me. That was the appeal, and also the risk I took on.

It turned out I could carry it. Two years later I had learned to write for people who have to defend the number to an auditor, not just to a client. I left in August 2026 for IDX.
