When Cube System ended in March 2024, I did not go straight into the next job. I took three months off on purpose: no schedule, no salary, just finding out whether I could work on my own and what freelance work actually asks for.

Midwave, Inc. was the first yes, in April 2024. It stayed a side job the whole way: from July 2024 the main one was FE Credit, and Midwave took the evenings and weekends until September 2026. Two jobs at once is a scheduling problem before it is anything else.

The useful part was watching how a small remote team decides what to build, with none of the meetings a bigger company uses to decide the same thing. Those three months taught me the rest: how to price work, how to say no, and how to keep a week from disappearing.
