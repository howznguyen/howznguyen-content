My first engineering job, from July 2021 to March 2024, at an outsourcing company. The first internship was here too, in 2022: the first time I sat inside a real software team instead of a project of my own.

Before that I had written code for assignments, for students, and for forums I ran myself. Here it had to survive being read by other people, and it had to be handed over to someone whose name I did not know.

What stayed with me is mostly habits: how to read a codebase you did not write, how to ask a question that actually gets answered, and how to ship something small instead of waiting to ship something perfect.
