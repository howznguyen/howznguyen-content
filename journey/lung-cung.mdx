Two firsts in one trip: my first time in Hà Nội, and my first trek, up to Lùng Cúng in Hà Giang.

The road in is already the trip. Terraces climb the hills on both sides of the bridge, and the mountains keep opening up in front of you.

The trail starts in forest and stays there. About halfway up there is a waterfall that splits into two streams over moss and broken rock.

<Image
  src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dkxhoyenc/image/upload/v1792486906/content/journey/hanoi-trek-trek-5803.jpg"
  alt="A waterfall splitting into two streams down a mossy rock face in dense green forest, with two trekkers on the path to the right."
  caption="The waterfall on the way up."
/>

Past it the light goes thin and green. That is where I found a single white flower lying on the leaf litter, fallen and still clean, lit by one patch of sun coming through.

<Image
  src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dkxhoyenc/image/upload/v1792486911/content/journey/hanoi-trek-trek-5886.jpg"
  alt="A white camellia-like flower with a yellow centre lying on dark leaf litter in deep forest shade, lit by a single patch of sunlight."
  caption="One flower, one patch of sun."
/>

Late in the day we reached the top of Lùng Cúng. The mist came over the ridge, the light went gold, and the clouds below ran out to the horizon like a sea. A few riders were still up there with their engines off, watching the sunset with us.

I keep a photo of every place I pass through. This trip earned its place: the first time north, the first mountain, and a view that paid back every step of the climb.
