I started the master's programme at HUIT in 2024, six years after my first degree at the same school, and I am doing it while working full time.

Studying and working at once changes what a lecture is for. A topic stops being abstract in the week it turns up in something I have to ship, and the questions I bring to class are the ones the job asked first.

It is also the reason the return to campus in 2026 did not feel like a return at all: I had never really left.
