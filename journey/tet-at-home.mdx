Tet is the one stretch of the year when the laptop stays closed. I rode the motorbike home, a few hundred kilometres with a stop at the coast on the way, and spent the holiday away from the terminal.

The ride is the point. No rush, no deadlines, no build queue: salt wind, pink granite, and water so blue no filter could fake it. Some things cannot be generated. You have to go and see them.
