iTrum started in December 2016 as a clean install: nothing carried over from the earlier attempts except what I had learned. The idea was the forum I wished I had found as a kid, where software, guides, courses and news were all worth reading.

  src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dkxhoyenc/image/upload/v1784184886/blog/from-a-pentium-pc-to-my-first-software/itrum-old-forum-interface.png"
  alt="The early iTrum forum interface, with sections for new posts, Ghost and Windows, courses and documents."
  caption="The first iTrum interface, back when the whole thing was one admin and a lot of hope."
/>

It ran for nine years, and it was never only mine. People I have still never met in person wrote articles, moderated threads, reported problems and kept it alive. A few of them I can name; there were more whose names I have lost.

The domain moved around more than I like to admit: itrum.net, itrum.vn, itrum.org, then back to itrum.net after I forgot to renew it once and someone else took it.
