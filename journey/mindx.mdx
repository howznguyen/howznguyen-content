I taught programming at MindX Technology School from April 2020 to June 2024, part-time for most of that stretch, to classes of secondary and high school students.

Teaching is the quickest way to find out what you do not actually understand. Explaining a loop or a function to someone seeing it for the first time leaves nowhere to hide behind vocabulary.

It also ran alongside everything else: the last years of university, then the first job, then the second. I stopped in June 2024.
