Two years at FE Credit went into risk management tooling: the systems that decide who gets credit, and the numbers behind those decisions. In August 2026 I closed that chapter and moved to IDX.

IDX is the digital transformation institute inside HUIT, the university I studied at. Same campus, other side of the desk. The work is AI engineering now, and the part I like most is that it is not only production code: it is also what students will learn from.

Leaving a stable role for a smaller team was the easy decision in hindsight. Going back to the place I studied, with something to build, is the part I did not plan for.
