When I got my first Mac, I went looking for the small tools that solve one specific annoyance. I searched every corner of the internet, and kept landing on the same lists of the same twenty apps.

Finding the one indie app that fixes your problem is the hard part, not finding a list. So macgrove started as the list I wanted for myself: every app opened and checked by hand, the price stated plainly, and a brew command to copy when there is one.

It went up on the 17th of September, 2026 with 212 apps. It has kept growing since, and nobody can pay to be in it.
