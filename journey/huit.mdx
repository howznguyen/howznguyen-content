I started in 2018 at what was then Ho Chi Minh City University of Food Industry. Partway through, the school was renamed: HUIT, the University of Industry and Trade.

The campus did not change, only the sign on the gate. I finished there and did not expect to be back.

Eight years later I am: since August 2026 I work at IDX, the digital transformation institute inside the same university.
