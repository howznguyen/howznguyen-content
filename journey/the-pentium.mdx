---
title: "The Pentium"
date: "2009-09-01"
description: "The first computer at home: a Pentium, an ADSL line, and no idea what the internet was."
---

Around the fourth grade the family got a computer: a Pentium, with an ADSL line. Until then I had no real idea what the internet was.

The machine was weak, and that turned out to be the useful part. I could not install whatever I wanted, so I spent the time trying to make it run better instead. That led to ready-made Windows images, and then to wanting to know how anyone made them.

I went online to play games, chat on Yahoo, watch YouTube and download software. The questions came later, and they are where all of this starts.
