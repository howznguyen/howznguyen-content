In 2017 I opened a YouTube channel for iTrum. I chose the topics, wrote the scripts, recorded and edited, with friends helping on the audio now and then.

I was not thinking about content production or brand building. I wanted the forum to have something useful to watch, and to look like a technology community rather than a folder of links.
