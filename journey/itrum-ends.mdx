---
title: "iTrum closes"
date: "2025-12-26"
description: "Nine years after it started, the forum shut down on 26 December 2025."
---

iTrum stopped on 26 December 2025. I had less and less time for it, and a community cannot be kept alive by dropping in occasionally.

The internet had changed as well. Forums were no longer where people went: the conversations had moved to Discord and Facebook groups, where replies arrive faster than any thread.

It did not end because I stopped caring about it. What it left behind stayed: how to run a server, how to handle an incident at two in the morning, how to write for an audience, and the people I met through it.
