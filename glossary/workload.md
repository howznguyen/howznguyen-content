# vi

Workload là một tác vụ, ứng dụng hoặc dịch vụ đang tiêu thụ tài nguyên máy tính như CPU, RAM, storage và network. Trong homelab này, Home Assistant và XPEnology là hai workload riêng.

Tìm hiểu thêm: [IBM - What is a workload?](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/workload).

# en

A workload is a task, application, or service that consumes computing resources such as CPU, memory, storage, and networking. In this homelab, Home Assistant and XPEnology are separate workloads.

Learn more: [IBM - What is a workload?](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/workload).
